# Tilt Roller

A three.js ball-rolling game for phones. Tilt the phone left/right to steer, tap or jiggle the phone up to jump. The view stays upright while you roll the phone; speed grows over time.
Every start generates a new track with banked berms, jumps, rings of fire, pits, vertical loops,
horizontal spirals, off-camber sections, earthquakes, fog banks and a finish line with flags.

Play: https://lacwuh.github.io/tilt-roller/ (open on the phone, tap START and allow motion access).
Desktop: arrow keys to steer, Space to jump.

## Modes
- **Ball Run**: roll the chrome ball through rings of fire, loops, spirals, pits and jumps.
- **Truck Race**: drive a monster truck against 2 AI monster trucks and 3 AI sports cars.
  Bicycle-model tyres with load transfer and power oversteer (drift), per-wheel spring-damper
  suspension (heave, pitch, roll), stability assist, bumping between vehicles.

## Controls
Tilt the phone or hold the left/right side of the screen to steer (a tap nudges, holding ramps to
full lock). Tap the middle, swipe up or jiggle the phone to jump. The view stays upright while you
roll the phone. Desktop: arrow keys and Space.
